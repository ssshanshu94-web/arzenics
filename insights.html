No Hype. Just Truth.

Uncomfortable Truths

About delivery, systems, and why most transformations fail — including AI.

Delivery & Process

Execution realities

Agile fails when teams optimize ceremonies instead of delivery accountability.

Velocity going up often means risk is being hidden, not removed.

Process breaks faster at scale than tools ever will.

Most delivery issues are ownership problems disguised as execution problems.

Translation: Teams don't need new rituals — they need clear responsibility.

Project Management

Control & visibility

Jira doesn't fix delivery; it only makes dysfunction visible.

Standups without decision ownership are just synchronized reporting.

Metrics without context create false confidence at the leadership level.

Process standardization matters more than team "agility" beyond a certain scale.

Translation: Control is designed, not tracked.

Cloud & Architecture

Infrastructure truths

Cloud migrations stall because operating models don't change.

Lift-and-shift delays clarity more than it saves effort.

Cost overruns happen when no one owns architecture decisions end-to-end.

Technical debt grows fastest where architectural ownership is fragmented.

Translation: Cloud problems are rarely technical.

AI, LLMs & Automation

No hype zone

AI doesn't fix broken processes — it accelerates them.

LLM adoption fails when teams treat prompts as strategy.

Automation without workflow clarity increases chaos, not efficiency.

Most AI initiatives fail because decision boundaries are undefined.

Productivity gains disappear when AI outputs aren't operationally integrated.

Translation: AI amplifies systems — good or bad.
