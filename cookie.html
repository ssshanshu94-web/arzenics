Cookie Policy

Last updated: February 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Arzenics Tech (“Arzenics”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website. It explains what these technologies are, why we use them, and your rights to control their use.

By continuing to use our website, you consent to the use of cookies in accordance with this Cookie Policy, unless you disable them through your browser settings.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, mobile phone, or tablet) when you visit a website. Cookies help websites function efficiently and provide information to the website owners.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

2.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as security, network management, and accessibility. Without these cookies, certain features of the website may not work correctly.

2.2 Performance and Analytics Cookies

These cookies collect information about how visitors use our website, such as pages visited, time spent on the site, and error messages. This data is aggregated and anonymous and helps us improve website performance and user experience.

2.3 Functional Cookies

Functional cookies allow the website to remember choices you make, such as language preferences or previously filled form data, to provide a more personalized experience.

3. Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services such as analytics tools, scheduling platforms, or embedded services that place cookies on your device. These third parties may collect information according to their own privacy and cookie policies.

Arzenics does not control the use of third-party cookies and recommends reviewing the respective policies of such providers.

4. How You Can Control Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent.

Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality and performance of our website.

5. Data Protection and Privacy

Any personal data collected through cookies is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy. We do not use cookies to collect sensitive personal information without your explicit consent.

6. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legal requirements, or our business practices. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Cookie Policy or our use of cookies, please contact us at support@arzenics.com.
